FAQ

Questions,answered.

Can't find what you're looking for?

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. We start from zero: what a pip is, how leverage works, how to read a candle. Then we build up step by step to advanced Smart Money Concepts. You don't need any prior experience.

What's the difference between Online and 1-on-1 Mentorship?

Both cover the full curriculum and include the certificate. Online Mentorship combines recorded lessons with live classes and community support. 1-on-1 Mentorship adds a personal mentor: private sessions, live market sessions together, every trade reviewed, a custom trading plan and lifetime support.

Are classes live or recorded?

Both. Live classes run online around the London and New York sessions, and every concept is also available as an HD recorded lesson so you can revise anytime.

Do you give signals or trade calls?

No. We teach you to find, plan and manage your own trades. Signals create dependency; skills create independence. Every live session is an education session where you see the reasoning behind each decision.

Can you guarantee I'll pass a prop-firm challenge?

No honest educator can guarantee that, because results depend on your discipline and execution. What we do is prepare you properly: a tested playbook, risk rules built around firm limits, and, for 1-on-1 students, a mentor guiding you phase by phase.

Which markets do you cover?

Our core focus is forex, both major and cross pairs, plus gold (XAUUSD), which most prop firms offer. Price action and Smart Money Concepts are universal, so the same skills transfer to indices and other markets.

Is forex trading legal in India?

Indian residents can legally trade currency derivatives, meaning INR pairs such as USD/INR and select cross pairs such as EUR/USD, on SEBI-recognised exchanges (NSE, BSE, MSE) through registered brokers. Trading forex on unauthorised overseas platforms is not permitted under FEMA, and the RBI publishes an Alert List of such platforms. Our programs are purely educational: we don't handle client funds or offer brokerage, and we encourage every student to follow the laws that apply to them.

How much time do I need to give?

Plan for 1–2 hours a day for lessons, backtesting and journaling. Live classes run in Indian afternoons and evenings around the London and New York sessions, so it works alongside a job or college.

In which language are classes taught?

Hindi and English. Concepts are explained in simple Hinglish, with all trading terminology in English so you can follow any global resource.

How do I enroll?

Choose a course and click enroll, or book a free counselling call. We'll understand your experience and goals and recommend the right path. 1-on-1 seats are limited each month.
